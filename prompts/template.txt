A template is one SVG drawing whose colours are SLOTS, so the game can repaint it in many colourways:
- fill/stroke may only be: {{main}} (biggest area), {{trim}} (second colour), {{accent}} (small details),
  {{outline}} (outline strokes), none, #ffffff (shine/highlights), #3a2a2a (eyes), #cdeeff (glass, windows),
  #7fd1ff (water).
- Use {{outline}} as the stroke on the main shapes with stroke-width="6" and stroke-linejoin="round".
- Allowed elements ONLY: svg, g, path, rect, circle, ellipse, polygon, polyline, line.
- Allowed attributes ONLY: xmlns, viewBox, d, x, y, width, height, rx, ry, cx, cy, r, points, x1, y1, x2, y2, fill,
  stroke, stroke-width, stroke-linecap, stroke-linejoin, opacity, fill-opacity, fill-rule. NO transform, NO text,
  NO gradients, NO style attribute. Every shape needs a fill (a slot or none).
- The root is <svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 256 256">, under 4000 characters, at most 60 shapes.
- Everything stays inside 0..256.

Two families of templates:

1. WEARABLES ({{wearables}}): drawn to fill a body "slot frame" (the 256x256 canvas IS the slot), front view.
   No size, no anchors. Must cover at least half of the frame in both width and height.
   - hair: the head is a circle centred (128,140) radius 90. Draw hair around/over the top of that circle;
     it may hang down the sides to y=256. Keep the face area x 75-181, y 125-215 clear.
   - top: the torso. Neck at top centre, shoulders at y=20 spanning x 20-236, hem at y=240. Short sleeves may
     reach the top corners. Front view, symmetric.
   - bottom: waist at y=0..30 across x 40-216, down to ankles at y=256. Trousers split into two legs below y=110
     (left leg x 40-124, right leg x 132-216). Skirts and shorts are one shape and end higher.
   - dress: shoulders at y=10 (x 40-216) down to the hem (y 200-256), flaring out.
   - socks: a pair. Left sock x 20-118, right sock x 138-236, from y=0 (top) to y=250 (toes pointing outwards).
   - shoes: a pair seen from the front-side. Left shoe x 10-120, right shoe x 136-246, soles flat at y=240,
     shoes start around y=110.
   - hat: sits on top of a head; the bottom edge (brim) is around y=200-240 and spans x 20-236.
   - glasses: two lenses centred at (78,128) and (178,128), joined by a bridge, arms going out to the edges.
   - bag: a bag seen from the front, filling the frame.

2. OBJECTS ({{objects}}): drawn standing on the ground or held, front view, centred,
   longest side filling at least 60% of the canvas (ideally 80%+), resting on the bottom of the canvas around y=230-250.
   - "size": {"w": number, "h": number} = real size in world units where an adult person is 100 tall.
     Size ranges for h by category: {{sizes}}.
   - "anchors": points [x, y] inside the drawing. Required per category: {{anchors}}.
     base = bottom centre where it rests on the floor (y at the bottom edge of the drawing).
     grip = where a hand holds it. Optional: seat (where a person sits, for chairs/sofas/beds/swings),
     top (a surface where small things can be put, for tables/shelves/counters; for a shelf unit, its highest shelf).
     A shelf unit with more levels also gives shelf1, shelf2, shelf3: the lower shelves, bottom to top, each a point
     at the centre of that shelf board's upper face, with at least 40 canvas units of empty space above each (the
     shelves are drawn EMPTY so the player puts things on them).
