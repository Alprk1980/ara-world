ARA World — the designer's own notes (she is a young girl; spelling fixed, wording kept):

- Lots of houses. Lots of stuff to decorate with.
- Babies, toddlers, kids, teens, adults and old people.
- Lots of different places to go to.
- Snowboarding place. Hidden places. All free. Schools/daycares. Important places.
- You can choose weather. When sun/moon is clicked (day/night).
- On top right corner you can make people, pets and clothes. Lots of tops, bottoms, socks, shoes, dresses and much
  more to choose from.
- On top left corner a back button.
- Bottom right corner a button to get characters out of the people bar.
- Pools, zoos, water animal zoo, theme park, rollercoaster place and waterpark.
- When a person is clicked emojis show; when an emoji is pressed the avatar feels that emotion.
- Airport with planes, VIP lounge and first class. Malls.
- Lots of places to eat, shop, play and much more. Sport play: sports arena.
- In bottom left a hammer: if hammer pressed, pick a place to build a house. There are thousands of houses to choose
  from. You can choose if your house has furniture in already or you can decorate. Pets. Halloween stuff.
- Post box place. Mansions. Beach houses. Lots of different clothes.
- School shopping place: backpacks, lunchboxes, pencil cases, bottles, uniform and other school stuff.
- When food or drinks are held up to the mouth, the food or drink gets teleported to the hand, the hand brings the food
  or drink to the mouth, the avatar eats/drinks.
- In the post box place every day you get a gift.
- There's also trains and buses. Football stadium.
- Every day a new thing adds to the house decorations. For gardens there are pools, kids stuff, fun stuff.
- There's all-age stuff for new babies, toddlers, kids, teens, adults and old people.
- There's an old people home. To go to different floors swipe up or down. VIP stuff for free.
- Supermarkets. Eye opticians. Hair dressers. Makeup shops. Makeup. Kingdom in the clouds.
- To play, open app: screen will be pink with roses and a play button.
- Hotels. Sweets shops. Toy shops. Restaurant. Offices.
- Vets. Cafes. Pub. Stables. Pet shop. Paint shop.
